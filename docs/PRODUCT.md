# BlueBase product demo

BlueBase is the digital home for an agent's business. Select and Plaza share a neutral workspace brand, while the personal profile identifies the agent's company, office, and market. The home screen answers: How am I doing? What am I working toward? What is coming next?

## Experience

The production headline has first priority; three supporting metrics establish context. A monthly actual/prior-year chart and pipeline strip tell the next part of the story. Annual goals remain annual when the production period changes. Goals show the current amount, target, percentage and remaining amount. A simple nine-month annualization explains volume pace. This is an illustration, not a predictive financial model.

Wealth Builder connects today's business to longer-term goals. Its confirmed contributions derive from closed sides, pending contributions from pending sides. The sample opening balance and prior contributions are explicitly explained in the module. Nothing transfers money.

Vendor browsing presents service partners as people agents can discover and remember, with search, categories, service areas and favorites. Fake contact details are shown for demonstration, without live call/email actions.

Events support professional learning and community connection. A Broker Open is an event with multiple participating listings, individual agents/brokerages, suggested stop order, host, sponsor and area. The route visualization is illustrative, not geocoded or suitable for navigation. RSVP is a local toggle; no event organizer is contacted. Calendar view supplements the list.

Tools demonstrates how the platform can grow. Future modules are visibly marked Coming soon and are not fake launch buttons.

All numbers, names, recommendations, event details and associations are fictional. Images are illustrative stock photos, not photos of the named properties or agent.

## Next product decisions

Before live implementation, define company membership, office scope and role semantics, production feed reconciliation and side attribution, the Wealth Builder program rules, vendor curation/moderation, event eligibility/RSVP capacity, and account support ownership. These are deliberate future product decisions, not hidden functionality in this demo.
